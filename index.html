Retirement House Analytics

An internal analytics tool for the Retirement House team.

Retirement House is a senior-creator content brand operated by Flight House (Flighthouse Media). Retirement House Analytics uses TikTok Login Kit to let the Retirement House TikTok account authorize read-only access to its own video list and stats, for internal performance reporting.

The app does not post, edit, or delete content, and does not access any other TikTok user's data. It is not offered to the public.

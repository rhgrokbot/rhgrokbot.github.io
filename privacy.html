Retirement House Analytics - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RH Analytics (also called Retirement House Analytics, the "App") handles information. The App is run by Retirement House, a senior-creator content brand operated by Flight House (Flighthouse Media) ("we," "us," or "our"). It is an internal tool used only by the Retirement House team.

1. What we collect

When the Retirement House TikTok account signs in through TikTok Login Kit, it authorizes read-only access to the following, and nothing else:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): such as display name, avatar, and account ID.
  • Account stats (user.info.stats): such as follower, following, like, and video counts.
  • Video list and stats (video.list): our own public videos and their metrics, such as views, likes, comments, and shares.

We do not collect data about other TikTok users, and we do not collect passwords. TikTok handles sign-in directly.

2. How we use it

We use this information only for internal performance analytics: tracking how our own content performs and preparing reports for the Retirement House team. The App does not post content or take any action on TikTok.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties. It is used only by the Retirement House team. We would disclose it only if required by law.

4. Storage and security

Metrics are stored privately in access-restricted systems used for the team's internal reporting. Access tokens are kept confidential and used only to fetch our own account's data. We take reasonable steps to protect this information, though no system is perfectly secure.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep metrics only as long as they're useful for internal reporting. You can ask us to delete stored data at any time by emailing the address below, and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions). Once access is revoked, the App can no longer retrieve any data.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by adult members of the Retirement House team.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: theretirementhouse@gmail.com.
